A LITTLE ABOUT ME

MORE THAN
JUST CODE.

I didn't start with a perfect roadmap. I started with curiosity.

I wanted to understand what happens behind the screen — how a website comes alive, how frontend communicates with backend, how data moves through an application, and how all those pieces become one working system.

That curiosity led me toward Full Stack Development. Along the way, cybersecurity caught my attention because building something useful is only part of the journey. Understanding how it can be protected is another.

Right now, I'm learning by building, experimenting, breaking things, fixing them and understanding the technology behind what I create.
